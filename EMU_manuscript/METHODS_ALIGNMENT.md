## 3. Verified Items Requiring No Code Change

- **Hippocampal ripple IED exclusion (`FigS5_ripples.ipynb`):** The within-HPC ripple detector uses a 25–60 Hz bandpass filter and $z > 4$ threshold for local IED masking (`IED_BAND = (25.0, 60.0)`, `IED_Z_THRESHOLD = 4.0`), which matches the *Hippocampal ripple detection* Methods subsection verbatim (distinct from the 25–80 Hz, 5-SD whole-montage preprocessing step in *Neural pre-processing*).
- **Parameter recovery restarts (`Fig1DEG_S1_TableS1_modeling_EMU.ipynb`):** Methods state *"each model was fit from three independent seeds, and the fit with the lowest negative posterior likelihood was retained"*, which corresponds to `N_STARTS = 3` random EM initialization seeds per simulated cohort (`N_SUBJECTS = 100`, `NTRIALS = 80`).
- **PPC & PSI surrogate normalization (`Fig5DEF_S13_PPC_PSI.ipynb`):** Uses `N_SURROGATES = 500` cut-and-reverse block-swapped surrogates per electrode pair and condition (`membandit.phase_sync.zscore_vs_evoked_surrogate`), matching the *Computing theta-phase synchrony* Methods subsection.
- **Figure S13 positive-RPE PPC correlations (`Fig5DEF_S13_PPC_PSI.ipynb`):** Section 6 correlates subject-mean HPC–ACC theta PPC during `positive` RPE recognition-cue trials against ACC RPE reactivation ($r = -0.325, p = 0.237$; reported as $-0.33$) and against ACC–HPC hit RSA ($r = 0.429, p = 0.111$; reported as $0.43$), matching Figure S13A–B.
